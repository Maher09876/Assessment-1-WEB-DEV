Poutine (Canadian Dish)

Poutine is a typical Canadian comfort food that has its origins in Quebec and is popular in the whole country. It consists of crispy golden fries with soft cheese curds and coated with hot and rich gravy that fuses all the ingredients. It is a dish that is famous due to its ombination of tastes and crunchy, creamy, and savory all combined in a bowl. Poutine is usually available in restaurants, diners and festivals and most establishments make their poutines by adding in toppings such as meat, onions or spices. It is easy to prepare, nutritious to consume and it is rated as one of the most symbolic foods in Canada.

Ingredients

  • Potatoes (cut into fries)
  • Cheese curds
  • Gravy
  • Salt
  • Oil for frying

Method

  1. Heat oil and fry the potatoes until golden.
  2. Place fries on a plate.
  3. Add cheese curds on top.
  4. Pour hot gravy over everything.
  5. Serve immediately and enjoy.
